# Glossary · 术语表

The terms of the Programme as the overview uses them, each with the overview's own sentence and its place in the book; the Chinese terms are those of the Chinese README. 本表列出总纲中使用的术语：英文、中文 README 所用的中文译名、总纲中的原句（英文）及其在书中的位置。

| Term | 中文 | In the overview's words | Where |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **The Grothendieckian Programme** | 格罗滕迪克纲领（The Grothendieckian Programme） | The Grothendieckian Programme is a programme for machine reasoning about mathematics, set out in seven works of the WiseMotif Monograph of Mathematical Intelligence. | Abstract |
| **recursive mathematical discovery** | 递归式数学发现 | A structure is proposed; it is tested against the cases in which the answer is known; what survives is abstracted into a new setting, and the question is posed again there. | §1.2 |
| **rising sea** | 涨潮（rising sea） | The model is Grothendieck's rising sea: a hard problem is not attacked where it stands; a theory is raised around it until the problem becomes natural and gives way (Series 03, Series Note). | §1.2 |
| **research subject** | 研究课题 | A research subject of the Programme is a problem on which the method is carried through in full, with every step recorded in a form that a mathematician can check and a machine can execute (Series 07, Series Note). | §4.3 |
| **centre and kernel** | 核心与内核 | The second work describes the arrangement in its own words: the Programme has a centre and a kernel (Series 02, Preface). | §4.2 |
| **world** | 世界 | It is a structured environment in which expressions are well formed, objects have declared types, morphisms have variance, locality has a specified meaning, constructions have universes and claims have admissible modes of verification. | §5.2 |
| **bridge** | 桥 | A bridge is a directed relation between worlds: a functor, an adjunction, a localisation, a realisation, a change of base, a comparison, a truncation, a completion or a reconstruction procedure. | §5.2 |
| **obstruction** | 障碍 | Obstructions are the dual unit — a failed lift, a non-zero class, an incompatible overlap, a missing inverse or a countermodel — and each says why an attempted construction remains open (Series 01, Integrated Preface). | §5.2 |
| **obligation** | 义务 | A bridge of the first work becomes a morphism of sites, and the question the first work asks of a bridge — what it preserves — becomes a question the second can answer by computing what the morphism carries across and what it loses; an obligation becomes an open cell of a contract, printed as open until it is discharged (Series 02, Preface). | §4.2 |
| **GMUS** | GMUS（格罗滕迪克数学宇宙系统） | The Grothendieck Mathematical Universe System, GMUS, is the system that proposes and investigates mathematical worlds (Series 01, Vol. III, Preface). | §5.4 |
| **GMLS** | GMLS（格罗滕迪克数学语言系统） | The Grothendieckian Mathematical Language System, GMLS, is the proposal side for mathematical language. | §5.4 |
| **coupled rising-sea cycle** | 耦合的 rising-sea 循环 | The exchange is a coupled rising-sea cycle: a world may reveal structures beyond the precision of the current language, and a richer language may expose distinctions that require a refined world or a new probe (Series 01, Vol. III, Preface). | §5.5 |
| **planes of authority** | 权威平面 | Authority is divided into five planes: mathematical meaning, proposal and discovery, certified computation, evidence and governance, and operations. | §5.3 |
| **site** | site（景） | A world of the first work presents its evidence as a site: its admissible pieces of evidence, the order in which they refine one another, and the families that cover a claim. | §4.2 |
| **sheaf** | 层 | The kernel of the second work places a sheaf of local information over that site and computes what glues. | §4.2 |
| **global section** | 整体截面 | A conclusion is a global section, and it exists only where the local information glues. | §6.2 |
| **cohomology class (of a failure to glue)** | 上同调类（粘合失败的） | Where it does not glue, the failure is not noise; it is a cohomology class — an obstruction with a definite location and a definite size, which a system can compute, report and act upon (Series 02, Preface). | §6.2 |
| **Topos-MoE** | Topos-MoE | On this footing the work builds nine layers over a single base, from the context site to the transfer layer, and a mixture of experts in which each expert is an open sub-topos, with its own local logic and sections, and routing is the construction of a sufficient cover rather than the choice of a fixed number of experts (Series 02, Abstract). | §6.3 |
| **status label** | 状态标签 | Every work of the Programme marks the status of each substantive claim where the claim is made, and no two of them use exactly the same set of marks. | §9.1 |
| **ledger** | 台账 | The fourth work gives every substantive assertion an identifier, a status, a dependency list and, where it applies, a formal statement, in a machine-readable ledger that ships with the source (Series 04, Statement of Epistemic Status). | §9.2 |
| **falsification test** | 证伪测试 | The third attaches ten falsification tests to the edges of its dependency graph and states as well what would not refute it (Series 03, Preface); the sixth specifies twenty-two critical experiments with predetermined refuting outcomes (Series 06, Preface); the seventh lists seventeen tests, five of them fatal (Series 07, Abstract); the second requires every module to name the measurement that would end it (Series 02, Preface). | §9.2 |
| **boundary** | 主张边界 | Each work of the Programme states its boundary in its first pages. | §10.1 |
