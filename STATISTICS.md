## Definitions


  formulas    display: \[…\], $$…$$, and the environments equation, align, gather, multline, eqnarray, flalign,
              alignat, displaymath (starred or not), one formula per display; inline: $…$ and \(…\). Excluded:
              code (verbatim, listings, Highlighting, \verb, Markdown code), comments, and drawings (tikzpicture),
              as Series 01's count_formulas.py. M1 = regular expressions (Series 01's definitions); M2 = a
              character-level scanner of math mode (an independent algorithm). Series 01: M2 = the native
              equations of its Word edition, as published.
  statements  numbered theorems, propositions, lemmas, corollaries, definitions and conjectures. M1 = the source
              (environments; S01 bold Markdown heads; S02 bold heads of the compiled LaTeX); M2 = bold statement
              heads printed in the PDF.
  algorithms  numbered algorithms. M1 = the source (algorithm environments, statement kind Algorithm); M2 = bold
              "Algorithm n" heads in the PDF.
  figures     figures and plates with a numbered caption. M1 = the List of Figures / Plates of the final build
              (.lof/.lop); M2 = bold "Figure n" / "Plate n" caption labels in the PDF.
  tables      tables with a numbered caption. M1 = the .lot of the final build; M2 = bold "Table n" labels.
  references  entries of the book's bibliography. M1 = the .bbl (biblatex / BibTeX) or the citeproc list;
              M2 = the count published in the work's release.yaml (S01, S02: no unified bibliography).
