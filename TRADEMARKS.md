# Names and Identity

"The Grothendieckian Programme" identifies this book, the seven works it overviews (Series 01–07 of the
WiseMotif Monograph of Mathematical Intelligence) and their author, Vincent Liu. Each of the seven has its own
repository and its own statement of how its names may be used. This policy explains how the name of the
Programme may be used, so that readers can tell the authentic works apart from independent work.

**You may, without asking:**

- refer to the Programme or to any of its works by name when citing, reviewing, teaching or discussing them;
- describe an independent project as "based on", "inspired by", or "implementing ideas from"
  *The Grothendieckian Programme*, with a citation.

**Please ask first before:**

- using the name of the Programme in the name of a product, project, organization, domain or social-media account;
- describing anything as "official", "endorsed", "certified" or "reference" with respect to the Programme or its
  works, or presenting a construction as "the" realization of one of their conjectures or open problems;
- using the cover art, the seven-cell honeycomb or the other visual identity of the series.

The adjective *Grothendieckian* names a mathematical method; it carries no attribution of design or endorsement
to Alexander Grothendieck (see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md), §4).
